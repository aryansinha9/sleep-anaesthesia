FAQ — dentists & practices

Everything your practice needs to know

Booking & schedulingHow far in advance do we need to book?+

We recommend booking at least 1–2 weeks in advance, but we do our best to accommodate urgent requests. Regular clients often secure recurring days each month.

Booking & schedulingHow many patients do we need to book?+

We typically need around 3–4 hours of operating time to make a session feasible. This equates to about $3,000–$4,000 worth of procedures — for example, four sets of wisdom teeth extractions or one full All-on-X surgery.

Booking & schedulingWhat areas do you service?+

We service dental clinics across:

  • All of Brisbane, the Gold Coast and the Sunshine Coast
  • Melbourne CBD and South East suburbs

If you're unsure whether you're in our coverage area, feel free to contact us.

Booking & schedulingHow does scheduling work — can you accommodate short-notice appointments?+

We aim to be flexible. While advance bookings are best, we can sometimes accommodate short-notice cases. Regular clinics get priority in our schedule.

Booking & schedulingHow long do you typically need for setup and recovery?+

We arrive 30–45 minutes before the first patient to set up. Most patients recover in 30–60 minutes, and we stay until they are safely discharged.

Booking & schedulingCan you sedate multiple patients in one day at our clinic?+

Absolutely. The more rooms and patients, the better. We can bring additional medical and nursing staff to streamline sedation, recovery, and patient flow for high-volume days.

Booking & schedulingWhat support do you need from our staff on the day?+

We need:

  • A clean treatment room with a dental chair
  • Power access
  • One trained dental assistant

We handle all sedation tasks, including patient monitoring and recovery.

Clinical & safetyWhat types of sedation do you provide?+

We specialise in intravenous (IV) conscious sedation for dental procedures. This allows patients to remain responsive but deeply relaxed. We regularly provide sedation for:

  • All-on-X surgery
  • Wisdom teeth extractions
  • Implants and other surgical procedures
  • Anxious patients, even for routine dental work
Clinical & safetyDo you bring your own equipment and drugs, or do we need to provide them?+

We are fully self-contained. We bring all sedation drugs, hospital-grade monitoring equipment, and emergency gear. Your clinic simply needs to provide a treatment room and dental staff for the procedure.

Clinical & safetyWhat qualifications and certifications do you have?+

We exclusively use ANZCA-accredited specialist anaesthesiologists. All sessions are also supported by a trained anaesthetic nurse or technician, ensuring high efficiency and optimal safety.

Clinical & safetyWhat monitoring do you perform during sedation?+

We continuously monitor oxygen saturation, blood pressure, ECG, respiratory rate, and consciousness level throughout the procedure. Our equipment and protocols meet hospital standards.

Clinical & safetyWhat are your emergency protocols?+

We are trained in advanced life support and carry full emergency kits. If a complication arises, we manage it immediately and coordinate with emergency services if needed. Fortunately, serious events are extremely rare.

Clinical & safetyWhat are the criteria for patient eligibility for IV sedation?+

Most healthy patients (ASA I–II) are suitable for IV sedation. We screen all patients through a medical questionnaire to assess risks. Higher-risk or complex medical cases may be referred for hospital-based care.

Clinical & safetyWhat's your protocol for fasting or pre-operative instructions?+

Patients must fast from solids for 6 hours and may have clear fluids up to 2 hours before sedation. They must arrange a responsible escort and avoid driving or legal decision-making for 24 hours post-procedure.

Clinical & safetyHow do you handle patient follow-up or post-op care?+

We provide all patients with written and verbal post-op instructions, and they are welcome to contact us if they have questions or concerns post-procedure.

Billing & adminDo you handle the patient consent process, or should we?+

We take care of IV sedation-specific consent, separate from your dental procedure consent. Consent is obtained in advance via our secure digital platform.

Billing & adminDo you provide sedation records for our clinical files?+

Yes. We supply comprehensive sedation records, including vital signs, medications used, times, and recovery status, for your records and compliance.

Billing & adminAre you insured and licensed to practise in our jurisdiction?+

Yes. All practitioners are fully insured, credentialed, and licensed to perform IV sedation in Queensland and Victoria.

Billing & adminDo you help with documentation for regulatory compliance or audits?+

Yes. We maintain complete records and are familiar with Dental Board of Australia, AHPRA, and local health authority guidelines. We're happy to support your compliance needs.

Billing & adminHow is your service billed — per case or per hour?+

We typically bill per patient. We process Medicare rebates directly for eligible services and provide patients with an invoice for any remaining out-of-pocket cost.

Billing & adminDo you bill the patient directly or the clinic?+

We bill the patient directly — there is no cost to your clinic. We process Medicare rebates for eligible services and provide patients with an invoice for any remaining out-of-pocket cost.

Billing & adminCan patients contact you directly before or after treatment?+

Yes. Patients can reach us through our online portal, text, or email for any pre- or post-sedation queries.

Billing & adminDo you provide patient instructions or forms in advance?+

Yes. All forms — including pre-op instructions, medical history, and consent — are sent in advance via our secure online system.

Billing & adminCan patients complete medical history or consent forms online?+

Yes. Our digital platform allows patients to complete and submit all necessary forms securely before their appointment.
