About us

Anaesthesiology services for IV dental sedation

Up to 40% of people experience dental fear. Sleep Anaesthesia features a team of qualified anaesthesiologists, assistants and nurses to enhance your dental experience.

We are a team of anaesthesiologists, anaesthetic assistants and recovery nurses. Our anaesthesiologists are medical specialists with over 15 years of training, accredited with ANZCA, the college of anaesthesia and pain medicine. We provide safe, quality anaesthetic sedation services at dental clinics using hospital-grade equipment.
